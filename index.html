POPPETCOVE

Publisher Privacy Policy

Whimsical printables for life's little joys

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Current status: PoppetCove Publisher is a private application under development. It is not yet authorized for live Pinterest publishing. The practices below describe its intended use upon authorization and deployment; any material changes will be reflected here.

1. About the application

PoppetCove Publisher is a private, owner-operated application intended to help the owner of PoppetCove prepare and publish original promotional Pins to the owner's own Pinterest business account. It is not offered as a service to the public.

2. Information processed

When authorized and connected, the application may process:

  • The authorized Pinterest account identifier and information about boards owned by that account.
  • OAuth access and refresh credentials needed for authorized Pinterest API requests.
  • Owner-provided promotional images, Pin titles, descriptions, links, and publication dates.
  • Owner approvals, configuration, and limited operational records used to prevent duplicates and investigate publishing outcomes.

The application is not designed to collect buyers' payment information, customer purchase details, or information about other Pinterest users.

3. Purposes

Information is used to authenticate the owner's account, identify appropriate owned boards, publish specifically approved Pins, verify results, maintain security, and avoid duplicate posts. Pinterest API data will not be used to train AI models or passed to an AI model provider.

4. Storage and security

The intended deployment operates locally on an owner-controlled Windows device. Credentials are designed to be encrypted with Windows security facilities and protected by an isolated owner-controlled account. Those security arrangements must be reviewed and established before live activation. Pinterest account, board, and Pin response content is intended to be processed transiently rather than archived. Limited owner-originated approval and operational records may be retained locally to support reliability and prevent duplicate publication.

5. Sharing and public content

The application communicates with Pinterest's official authorization and API services for approved operations. It does not sell account information or provide Pinterest API data to advertising networks or AI model providers. Approved promotional Pins and their destinations become publicly visible on Pinterest after successful publication. Pinterest independently processes data according to its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Retention, access, and deletion

Locally stored configuration, credentials, approvals, and operational records are retained as needed for operation, security, and duplicate prevention. The owner can stop publishing, revoke the app's Pinterest authorization, and delete local application data. Revocation or local deletion does not remove Pins already published; those must be managed on Pinterest. Retention procedures will be reviewed before live deployment.

7. No cookies or advertising tracking

The local publishing connector does not operate its own advertising-tracking cookies, ad campaigns, or cross-site tracking. Pinterest's own login and website practices are governed by Pinterest.

8. Changes

This notice will be updated if the application, its data handling, or applicable requirements materially change.

9. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to PoppetCove using the Etsy shop contact/message option. A dedicated business email may be added to this notice later.
